Your uploaded study notes are embedded in isolated vectors scoped strictly to your authenticated user ID. No other user can access, read, or query your private documents.

We use official Google OAuth and one-time passwordless email verification codes. Passwords, auth secrets, and credentials are never stored in plain text.

You may request full removal of your documents, quiz history, and account profile at any time through settings or by contacting our team.
